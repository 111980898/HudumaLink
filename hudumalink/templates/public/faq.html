Answers before you need them.

Practical guidance for reporting, tracking, and closing a service request.

01

Keep your reference.

Every submitted request gets a tracking number. Save it somewhere easy to find. You can use it on the Track page without signing in.

Go to tracking ↗
How do I report a problem?

Create a free account, choose Report a problem, pick the service category, add a location and describe what is happening. A tracking number appears as soon as the request is submitted.

Can I track without signing in?

Yes. Enter the reference on the Track page. The public view shows the service, sub-county, status, progress history, and any public update without exposing personal details.

What do the statuses mean?

Pending means received and waiting for review. In Progress means assigned to a department. Resolved means the work is complete. Rejected means the request is outside scope or a duplicate, with the reason shown in the notes.

How long should a request take?

Each service has a target response time. Cases that pass their target are flagged to staff as overdue so they can be followed up.

Is my information public?

No. Public pages show only a reference, service, sub-county, subject, and status. Your name, email, phone, and internal notes stay private.

What photos can I upload?

PNG, JPG, GIF, or WEBP files up to 5 MB. Upload a clear photo that helps the service team locate or understand the issue.

I forgot my password.

For now, contact the service desk from the support link above and include the email address on your account. Never share your current password.
