Help & frequently asked questions

How do I report a problem?

Create a free account, choose Report a problem, pick a category, say where it is, describe it and optionally add a photo. You will get a tracking number straight away.

How do I know what is happening with my request?

Open My requests for a full timeline, or enter your tracking number on the Track page — no sign-in required. You also get an in-app notification each time the status changes.

What do the statuses mean?

Pending — received, waiting for review. In Progress — assigned to a department. Resolved — the work is done. Rejected — outside county services or a duplicate; the reason is shown in the notes.

How long should it take?

Each service has a target (for example water problems in 3 days). Cases past their target are flagged to the administrators as overdue.

Is my information public?

No. The public tracking page and activity feed show only the reference, service type, sub-county and status. Your name, email and phone stay private.

What photos can I upload?

PNG, JPG, GIF or WEBP up to 5 MB. We check the actual file contents, not just the name.

I forgot my password.

This prototype has no email recovery. Ask a county administrator to help, or create a new account.
